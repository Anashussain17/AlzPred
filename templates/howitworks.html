How AlzPredict Works

Follow these simple steps to understand our Alzheimer's detection process.

Step 1: Complete the Questionnaire

After logging in, you are prompted to answer a series of dynamic questions about your cognitive functions and memory. Your responses help us assess your risk level.

Step 2: Risk Evaluation

Your responses are analyzed to compute a risk score. If your score exceeds a predefined threshold, you'll be prompted to upload your brain MRI scan for further analysis.

Step 3: Upload Your MRI Scan

If the risk evaluation indicates a potential issue, you'll be directed to the MRI upload page. Here, you can securely upload your brain MRI scan.

Step 4: Image Preprocessing

The uploaded MRI scan is automatically preprocessed—resized, normalized, and validated—to ensure it is suitable for analysis.

Step 5: Diagnosis Prediction

Our customized ResNet50-based deep learning model analyzes the preprocessed MRI scan and predicts the stage of Alzheimer's disease.

Step 6: Receive Your Results

Finally, your diagnosis and feedback are displayed on the results page. You can then consult with a healthcare professional based on these insights.
