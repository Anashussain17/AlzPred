Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Find answers to common questions about Alzheimer's disease and how AlzPredict helps in early detection.

AlzPredict is an AI-powered system designed to assist in the early detection of Alzheimer’s disease using deep learning and MRI scans. It helps medical professionals analyze brain scans and identify early signs of Alzheimer's more accurately.

AlzPredict uses deep learning models trained on MRI images of the brain to detect patterns associated with Alzheimer's disease. The model processes the scan and classifies it into different stages of Alzheimer's, assisting doctors in making an early diagnosis.

Early detection allows patients to receive timely treatment, lifestyle adjustments, and medical interventions that can slow disease progression and improve quality of life.

AlzPredict analyzes structural MRI (sMRI) scans, which provide detailed images of brain structures. These scans help detect atrophy and changes in brain volume associated with Alzheimer’s disease.

AlzPredict processes MRI scans within a few seconds to a couple of minutes, depending on the scan's resolution and system performance.

AlzPredict is designed for use by healthcare professionals, neurologists, and researchers specializing in Alzheimer’s disease diagnosis and treatment.
