About AlzPredict

Early Detection of Alzheimer's Disease Using Deep Learning

What is Alzheimer's Disease?

Alzheimer's disease is a progressive brain disorder that slowly destroys memory, thinking ability, and the ability to perform daily activities. It is the most common cause of dementia, affecting millions of people worldwide.

The disease starts with mild memory loss and gradually worsens over time, leading to severe cognitive impairment. Early detection is crucial for better management and treatment.

Why AlzPredict?

According to the World Health Organization (WHO), over 50 million people worldwide are living with dementia, and this number is expected to reach 152 million by 2050. Alzheimer's disease accounts for 60-70% of these cases.

A 2018 report by Alzheimer’s Disease International found that only 1 in 4 people with Alzheimer’s are diagnosed in low- and middle-income countries due to limited access to advanced diagnostic tools.

Our AI-powered system aims to bridge this gap by providing a cost-effective, accessible, and accurate early detection method for Alzheimer's disease.

Causes of Alzheimer's Disease

Alzheimer’s disease occurs due to the abnormal buildup of proteins in and around brain cells. Two key proteins involved are:

  • Beta-amyloid plaques: Clumps of this protein form between nerve cells, disrupting communication.
  • Tau tangles: Inside brain cells, tau proteins twist abnormally, preventing nutrients from being transported properly.

Symptoms and Progression

The disease progresses in three main stages:

  • Mild (Early Stage): Short-term memory loss, difficulty solving problems, and losing track of time.
  • Moderate (Middle Stage): Increased confusion, difficulty recognizing people, and mood changes.
  • Severe (Late Stage): Loss of communication, full-time care needed, and severe physical decline.

Current Treatments and Management

Although there is no cure, medications like Donepezil and Memantine can help manage symptoms. Lifestyle changes, therapies, and early intervention play a crucial role in slowing progression.

Importance of Early Detection

Detecting Alzheimer’s early helps in better treatment planning. AI-based systems like AlzPredict assist in early detection using MRI scans, making diagnosis faster and more accessible.

The Future of AlzPredict

AlzPredict leverages deep learning to identify Alzheimer's stages from MRI scans, assisting doctors in early diagnosis. Our system aims to enhance patient care and provide accurate results, helping individuals receive timely intervention and treatment.

Future enhancements could improve accuracy and extend the model’s capabilities to detect other brain-related disorders.
